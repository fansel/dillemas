Conclusion

What we conclude

The dilemma

1

Every option gives something up. Protecting children and protecting private communication are both legitimate goals.

Our position

2

Chat Control 2.0 conflicts with freedom, fairness and transparency. Harm and benefit remain open.

3

The Parliament’s targeted model does better on those three, but it only finds people who are already suspected.

4

Our weighing: certain, widespread costs weigh more than an unproven benefit.

Who may read our messages? Not everyone, all the time. A judge, and a concrete suspicion.
