Why privacy matters

“I’ve got nothing to hide”

The most common argument for surveillance, and three problems with it (Solove, 2007; Schneier, 2006)

Small facts add up

Harmless data points, combined, show health, beliefs, relationships and routines. “Give me six lines by the most honest man, and I’ll find enough to hang him.” (Richelieu)

Privacy is not secrecy

We have blinds on our windows and a door on the bathroom without being criminals. “No different than saying you don’t care about free speech because you have nothing to say.” (Snowden)

Rules change

What is harmless today can become a problem under a future government or in another country.

Solove, D. J. (2007). “I’ve Got Nothing to Hide” and Other Misunderstandings of Privacy. San Diego Law Review, 44, 745. Schneier, B. (2006). The Eternal Value of Privacy. Snowden: Reddit AMA (2015).
