Why privacy matters

Access built for the police can be used by others

Greece, 2004–05

The Athens Affair

Unknown attackers used the legal wiretap function in Vodafone Greece’s network to listen to about 100 phones, including the Prime Minister’s.

USA and worldwide, 2024–25

Salt Typhoon

Chinese state hackers got into US telecom networks, including systems used for court-ordered wiretaps. In 2025, 13 countries reported 200+ US organisations affected.

In Dec 2024 the FBI and CISA advised Americans to use encrypted messengers.

Security researchers conclude that a way in that works only for authorities cannot be built (Abelson et al., 2015).

Prevelakis & Spinellis (2007), IEEE Spectrum; joint advisory of 13 countries (27 Aug 2025); Abelson et al. (2015), Keys under doormats
